# Weekly Log

##### Effective 2026-09-16

A weekly log should be kept. It is a record of what was planned for the week versus what was accomplished. It should be polished and ready to be published and use the following format:
YYYY-MM-DD (the day should be a Monday)
Goals
Total hours worked
Accomplishments
Key Findings
Problems
Looking Ahead

Weekly logs should be kept in markdown files.
